Frequently Asked Questions

Clear expectations for mobile interior detailing with TopNotch DetailLab.

Is this a mobile service?

Yes. TopNotch DetailLab is a mobile interior detailing specialist. Service is performed at your location with safe parking.

Is travel included?

Travel is included in package pricing. There is no separate travel fee for service within Indianapolis and surrounding Central Indiana communities.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. Customers never provide water or electricity. The service is fully self-contained.

What does “From” pricing mean?

“From” pricing reflects typical interiors. Heavily neglected or Recovery-level conditions may require custom assessment and adjusted pricing.

Are bookings automatically approved?

No. Bookings remain pending until reviewed and approved. Recovery Reset requests especially require review and may be declined if unsafe.

Why are Recovery photos required?

Recovery Reset involves heavy neglect, severe staining, strong odors, or major contamination. Photos are required to assess safety and scope before approval.

What about weather?

Weather is considered when scheduling mobile service. Severe weather may require rescheduling to protect both your vehicle and the detailing process.

Do I need safe parking?

Yes. A safe, accessible parking location is required for mobile interior detailing.

Should I remove valuables?

Yes. Please remove valuables and personal items from the interior before your appointment. Trash removal is included, but valuables should be removed by you.

What about child seats?

Child seats can remain installed, but access around them may be limited. If you prefer deeper cleaning under or around seats, you may remove them before service.

Can every stain be removed?

No. There is no guarantee every stain can be fully removed. Extraction and cleaning are performed thoroughly, but some stains may be permanent.

Can every odor be removed?

No. Odor treatment is performed, but strong or deeply embedded odors may not be fully removable.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment is accepted after service via Cash, Apple Pay, or PayPal.

What work is declined?

Biohazards, active mold remediation, needles, pest infestations, and hazardous materials are declined. Unsafe jobs are not performed.

Is cancellation flexible?

Yes. Cancellation and rescheduling are flexible when communicated in a reasonable timeframe.
