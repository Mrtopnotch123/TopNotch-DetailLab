Frequently asked questions

Know what to expect.

Everything you need to know about TopNotch DetailLab services, pricing, booking, and what happens at your appointment.

Are you mobile?

Yes. TopNotch DetailLab travels to your location — home, work, apartment complex, or another approved location — in Indianapolis and surrounding areas. You do not need to bring your vehicle anywhere.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. TopNotch DetailLab arrives fully self-contained with all the water and power needed to perform the service. You do not need to supply anything.

Is the service interior only?

Yes. TopNotch DetailLab specializes exclusively in interior detailing. Exterior washing, polishing, paint correction, and exterior protection are not offered.

How long does a service take?

Timing depends on the selected package, vehicle size, interior materials, and interior condition. A Quick Reset on a well-maintained sedan will take less time than a Deep Reset on a large SUV. An accurate time estimate is provided during confirmation.

Are the listed prices final?

Listed prices are starting prices based on a standard passenger vehicle in typical condition. Final pricing may vary based on vehicle size, interior materials, and the actual condition observed. The final price is always confirmed before work begins — you will never be surprised after the fact.

When is payment due?

Payment is due after the service has been completed and the final amount has been confirmed. Payment details and accepted methods are provided during the confirmation process.

Does submitting the booking form confirm my appointment?

No. Submitting the form prepares a booking request. Your appointment is not confirmed until TopNotch DetailLab has reviewed your request and the service, final price, location, date, and time have all been confirmed.

What if I need to reschedule?

Rescheduling details will be provided as part of the appointment confirmation. Please contact TopNotch DetailLab as soon as possible if your availability changes before the appointment is completed.

What is the service area?

TopNotch DetailLab serves Indianapolis and the surrounding areas. When you submit your booking request, include your city or ZIP code and TopNotch will confirm whether your location is within the service area.

What is a Recovery Assessment?

Recovery is TopNotch DetailLab's condition-based service for interiors that require substantially more labor than a standard preset — due to severe neglect, excessive trash, heavy embedded pet hair, persistent odors, widespread staining, or multiple severely affected areas. No fixed price is provided instantly. Clear interior photos are required before any pricing is delivered.

Why are interior photos required for Recovery?

Interior photos allow TopNotch DetailLab to accurately assess the condition and labor requirements before committing to a price. Providing photos protects both you and TopNotch — you receive an accurate quote, and TopNotch can prepare the right tools and time allocation for your vehicle.

Will every stain come out?

Not necessarily. Extraction and cleaning aim for the best achievable safe improvement, but age, dye transfer, chemical damage, discoloration, and permanent material damage may remain. TopNotch DetailLab will not make false guarantees about stain removal outcomes.

Can odor removal be guaranteed?

No. Odor treatment is presented as reduction, not guaranteed permanent elimination. Results depend on the odor source, how deeply it has penetrated the vehicle, and whether the source can be safely addressed. Persistent strong odors may require a Recovery Assessment.

Is pet hair included in standard services?

Normal loose pet hair may be vacuumed as part of the selected service. Light or moderate embedded pet hair requires the appropriate service selection in Build Your Own. Heavily embedded or extensive pet hair may require a Recovery Assessment before a fixed price can be provided.

What should I remove before the appointment?

Please remove all valuables, documents, personal belongings, and child seats before the appointment. TopNotch DetailLab removes all trash as part of any selected service, so loose trash does not need to be removed in advance.

Do I need to remove child seats?

Yes. Please remove child seats before the appointment. TopNotch DetailLab does not reinstall or adjust child safety restraint systems.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Weather-related scheduling adjustments are handled as part of the appointment confirmation and communication process. If weather affects your scheduled appointment, TopNotch DetailLab will work with you to reschedule.

Does the parking location matter?

Yes. The vehicle should be parked in a stable, accessible location with enough space to work around it safely. Include any relevant parking notes in the notes field when you submit your booking request.

What vehicles or conditions may be declined?

TopNotch DetailLab reserves the right to decline work involving biohazards, bodily fluids, active mold remediation, pest infestations, needles, hazardous materials, or any condition that poses an unreasonable safety risk. This determination is made at TopNotch's discretion and may occur before or during the service.
