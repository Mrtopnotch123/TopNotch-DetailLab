Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before we touch your vehicle.

Are you mobile?

Yes. TopNotch DetailLab travels to approved locations in Indianapolis and nearby Central Indiana communities.

Is there a travel fee?

No separate travel fee is added. Travel within the TopNotch DetailLab service area is included in Reset pricing.

Do I provide water or electricity?

No. TopNotch DetailLab arrives self-contained with the water and power needed for the service.

Why do Full and Deep Reset say “From”?

Final pricing depends on vehicle size, condition, materials, and required labor. The service and final price are confirmed before work begins.

Does a booking request confirm my appointment?

No. Requests remain pending until service, date, location, availability, and final price are reviewed and confirmed.

Why does Recovery require photos?

Recovery pricing depends heavily on condition. Clear interior photos are required before a price or appointment can be confirmed.

What happens in bad weather?

Mobile appointments may be rescheduled when weather or working conditions prevent safe, quality service.

What parking space is required?

The vehicle must be legally parked in a safe location with enough room to open doors and use service equipment.

What should I remove first?

Please remove valuables, documents, personal belongings, and child seats before service.

Can every stain or odor be removed?

No. Safe treatment aims for the best reasonable improvement, but permanent staining, material damage, dye transfer, and deeply embedded odors may remain.

Which payments are accepted?

Payment is due after service. PayPal, cash, and Apple Pay are accepted.

What conditions are declined?

Biohazards, bodily fluids, active mold remediation, pest infestations, needles, and hazardous materials are not accepted.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Contact TopNotch DetailLab as soon as possible when plans change.
