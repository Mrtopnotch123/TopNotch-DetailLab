Frequently asked questions

Know what to expect.

Are you mobile?

Yes. TopNotch DetailLab travels to approved locations in Indianapolis and surrounding areas.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. TopNotch DetailLab arrives with the water and power needed to perform the service.

Does requesting an appointment confirm it?

No. The appointment is official only after the date, time, location, service, and final price are confirmed.

How long does a service take?

Timing depends on the selected package, vehicle size, interior materials, and condition.

Will every stain come out?

No. Extraction and treatment aim for the best safe improvement, but age, dye transfer, chemical damage, discoloration, and permanent material damage may remain.

Is pet hair included?

Normal loose hair may be vacuumed. Embedded or excessive pet hair requires the Pet Hair Removal add-on or a Recovery assessment.

Can odor removal be guaranteed?

No. Results depend on the odor source and how deeply it has entered the vehicle. The service is presented as odor treatment and reduction, not guaranteed permanent elimination.

What should I remove before the appointment?

Please remove valuables, documents, personal belongings, and child seats. TopNotch removes all trash as part of the selected service.

What vehicles may be declined?

Vehicles involving biohazards, bodily fluids, active mold remediation, pests, needles, or hazardous materials may be declined.

When is payment due?

Payment is due after the service has been completed and the final amount has been confirmed.
