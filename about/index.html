About TopNotch DetailLab

Mobile interior detailing in Indianapolis built around honest expectations, careful work, and a result worthy of the name.

Who we are

Focused on the interior. Serious about the result.

TopNotch DetailLab is a mobile interior detailing service based in Indianapolis. Every service, every tool, and every interaction is built around one thing: a clean interior done right.

Interior Only

TopNotch DetailLab specializes exclusively in interior detailing. No exterior washing, no paint correction, no exterior protection. Every decision — from equipment to technique — is optimized for the inside of your vehicle, because that is the space you actually use every day.

Honest Expectations

Not every stain can be removed. Not every odor can be permanently eliminated. Not every surface can be fully restored. TopNotch DetailLab will never make a false promise just to win your business. You will know before work begins what is realistic and what is not.

Self-Contained Service

TopNotch DetailLab arrives with everything needed to complete your service. You do not need to provide water, electricity, or equipment. We come to your home, your workplace, or another approved location and handle everything from start to finish.

Final Inspection

Every completed service receives a final inspection before the vehicle is returned to the customer. The job is not done until the interior meets the TopNotch standard — not a lower bar set by time pressure or convenience.
