Five Levels of Interior Care

A fair package for every vehicle.

Clear service levels give you control over your budget while protecting the time and labor required for demanding interiors.

Budget Maintenance

Quick Clean

For lightly used vehicles that only need the essential basics.

Cars starting at $35 SUV/Truck $45 · 3-Row/Van $55
  • Quick vacuum of exposed seats and floors
  • Dashboard wipe-down
  • Center-console wipe-down
  • Door-panel wipe-down
  • Reasonable trash removal
Not included: Windows, floor-mat washing, detailed cup holders, stain treatment, pet-hair removal, steam cleaning or extraction.

Estimated time: 25–40 minutes

Routine Upkeep

Refresh

A complete maintenance clean for an interior already in fair condition.

Cars starting at $70 SUV/Truck $85 · 3-Row/Van $100
  • Thorough vacuum of seats and floors
  • Dashboard, console and door panels
  • Cup holders and accessible crevices
  • Interior-window cleaning
  • Floor mats vacuumed and cleaned
  • Light deodorizing finish

Estimated time: 1–1.5 hours

Deep Clean

Cars starting at $120 SUV/Truck $145 · 3-Row/Van $165
  • Everything included in Refresh
  • Detailed crevice and vent cleaning
  • Deep cleaning of plastics and vinyl
  • Material-appropriate seat cleaning
  • Carpet agitation and light spot treatment
  • Steam-assisted cleaning where material-safe
  • Door-jamb wipe-down
  • Interior protectant and final inspection

Estimated time: 2–3.5 hours

Intensive Restoration

Restoration

For staining, buildup, pet hair and interiors overdue for serious attention.

Cars starting at $180 SUV/Truck $210 · 3-Row/Van $240
  • Everything included in Deep Clean
  • Carpet extraction when appropriate
  • Cloth-seat extraction when appropriate
  • Advanced stain treatment
  • Moderate pet-hair removal
  • Moderate odor treatment
  • Leather conditioning when applicable
  • Extended labor on neglected areas

Estimated time: 3.5–5.5 hours

Severe-Condition Service

Recovery

For interiors requiring extensive labor, repeated treatment or a customized recovery plan.

Starting at $275 Photo review required
  • Severe embedded pet hair
  • Heavy grime and long-term buildup
  • Heavy staining or multiple spills
  • Strong smoke or persistent odor concerns
  • Excessive trash and extended labor
  • Multiple extraction or treatment rounds
Safety notice: Mold, active pests, needles, bodily fluids and other potential biohazards require separate assessment and may be declined.
