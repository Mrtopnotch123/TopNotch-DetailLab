How It Works

Simple, honest steps from choosing a service to final inspection—without instant confirmations or unrealistic promises.

1. Choose a preset or Build Your Own

Start with Quick Reset, Full Reset, Deep Reset, or Recovery—or build a custom package using the builder. The system will recommend better-value presets when appropriate.

2. Submit a pending booking request

Use the Book Now page to send a request with your vehicle details, interior condition, and notes. Recovery requests require clear interior photos before pricing.

3. Review, approval, and final inspection

Requests remain pending until reviewed and confirmed. On the day of service, safe parking, weather, and realistic expectations are confirmed before work begins, followed by a final interior inspection.
